\# SUZUKI - Visual Rules



\## PHOTOREALISM LOCK ⚠️



\### Master Rule

Suzuki must always appear as a real human being photographed in the real world.



\### Required Styles

\- ✅ Photorealistic

\- ✅ Documentary photography

\- ✅ Editorial photography

\- ✅ Music magazine photography

\- ✅ Concert photography

\- ✅ Studio portrait photography

\- ✅ Professional publicity photography

\- ✅ DSLR camera realism

\- ✅ Natural lighting

\- ✅ Real skin texture

\- ✅ Realistic pores

\- ✅ Realistic eyes

\- ✅ Realistic hands

\- ✅ Authentic environments

\- ✅ Real-world proportions



\### Camera References

\- Canon EOS R5

\- Sony A7R IV

\- Nikon Z8



\### Image Goal

The final image should be indistinguishable from a professional photograph of a real media personality working in the music industry.



\*\*Never produce artwork that looks AI-generated.\*\*

\## IDENTITY PRESERVATION LOCK



\### Preserve Always

1\. Facial structure

2\. Age appearance (36)

3\. Ethnic features (Thai-Japanese)

4\. Eye colour (dark brown)

5\. Hair colour (hot-pink)

6\. Hair style (dreadlocks, half-up)

7\. Silver beads and rings in dreadlocks

8\. Shirt artwork (FOOL FOR A GOOD TIME)

9\. Character identity



\### Never Replace With

\- Generic influencer

\- Generic social media personality

\- Generic model

\- Generic singer

\- Generic fashion model

\- Generic celebrity



\*\*Identity consistency is more important than scene creativity.\*\*



If a conflict exists between a prompt and this knowledge base, the knowledge base wins.
