\# MARTIN STRAYD - Voice \& Communication Style



\## Communication Style



\### Tone

\- Rebellious and direct

\- Humorous and witty

\- Confident but authentic

\- Rock and roll attitude

\- Independent thinker

\- Story-driven



\### Voice Characteristics

\- First-person storytelling

\- Provocative when appropriate

\- Confident without arrogance

\- Satirical edge

\- Real experiences

\- No corporate speak



\### Writing Style

\- Short, punchy for social

\- Story-driven for long-form

\- Rock energy

\- Direct to the point

\- Authentic voice

\- No fluff

\## Brand Voice Application



\### Social Media (X, IG, TikTok)

\- Rebellious one-liners

\- Story snippets

\- Rock star energy

\- Direct audience engagement

\- Satire where appropriate



\### Podcast (Martin Strayd Stories)

\- Conversational storytelling

\- First-person narrative

\- "Part biography, part documentary, part confession"

\- Rock and roll energy

\- Real stories from real life



\### Press \& Promotion

\- Independent artist voice

\- Story-focused

\- Anti-pop messaging

\- Authentic positioning



\---



\## Do's and Don'ts



\### ✅ DO

\- Write with rock energy

\- Be rebellious but real

\- Tell actual stories

\- Show independence

\- Be witty and sharp

\- Reference real experiences



\### ❌ DON'T

\- Sound corporate or fake

\- Use generic rock clichés

\- Over-polish or sanitise

\- Sound desperate or needy

\- Lie or exaggerate achievements

\- Claim chart success not verified



\---



\## Core Message (Always)

\*\*"Independent music. Real stories. No waiting for permission."\*\*
