\## Process

1\. Get casual photo

2\. Upload to Grok or headshot AI

3\. Generate professional headshot

4\. Save to `headshots/results/`

5\. Update character file if approved



\## Tips

\- Use clear, well-lit photo

\- Rock star aesthetic (leather, stage look)

\- Match character's established visual

\- NO anime/cartoon style
