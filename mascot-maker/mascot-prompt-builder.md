\# MASCOT MAKER AGENT



\## Purpose

Design the ultimate mascot for STRAYD brand.



\## Mascot Requirements



\### Style

\- Rock and roll

\- Professional but edgy

\- Memorable silhouette

\- Works at any size (icon to poster)



\### Character Traits (from Suzuki's story)

\- "Grew up the hard way"

\- "Made it through the cracks"

\- "Better than being dead"

\- Wit and attitude

\- Survivor energy



\### Visual Direction

\- Rock star aesthetic

\- Australian outback meets rock club

\- Koala skull motif (existing brand)

\- NOT generic - must be unique



\## Workflow

1\. Dump mascot ideas in `mascot\_ideas.md`

2\. Dahl generates mascot prompt

3\. Colab generates image

4\. Save to `approved\_mascots/`

5\. Update brand guide
